# MyJournal

A voice-first personal journal for Android. You speak or type; it keeps a private diary, answers
questions about it, and can pull TODOs out of what you wrote.

It has **no server and no account**. Your journal is stored encrypted on your phone and nowhere
else, speech is transcribed on the device, and nothing leaves it unless you explicitly turn on the
optional AI features and connect your own provider account.

- **[Privacy Policy](privacy/)** — what the app stores, what it sends, and what it never touches.
  Also in [Português (Brasil)](privacy/pt/).
- **[Support](support/)** — how to report a bug, ask a question, or report a security problem.

MyJournal is currently in **internal testing**; it is not yet publicly available on Google Play.
