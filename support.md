# Support

MyJournal is built and maintained by one person. There is no support desk and no ticket system —
email is the whole channel, and it reaches the developer directly.

**leonardoschluter@gmail.com**

## Reporting a bug

Email the address above. What helps most:

- What you were doing, and what happened instead.
- Your Android version and phone model.
- The app version, if you know it.

**Please do not paste your journal entries into a bug report.** The developer has no access to your
data and would rather keep it that way — describe the problem instead of showing the content. If a
specific entry is involved, say "an entry containing an accented character" rather than quoting it.

## Reporting a security problem

Security reports go to the same address: **leonardoschluter@gmail.com**. Please include enough
detail to reproduce the issue, and give a reasonable window for a fix before disclosing it publicly.

In scope: anything that lets someone read journal data they should not be able to, defeats the
database encryption or the app lock, leaks the API key, or causes journal text to be transmitted
without the consent the app asked for.

Out of scope: attacks that require a rooted or otherwise compromised device with the screen
unlocked. The [privacy policy](../privacy/#security-and-its-limits) is explicit that app-level
encryption cannot defend against that, and says so rather than implying otherwise.

There is no bug-bounty programme. This is a personal project with no revenue.

## Your data

Everything about what is stored, what is sent, and how to delete it is in the
**[Privacy Policy](../privacy/)** ([Português](../privacy/pt/)). The short version: it is all on
your device, you can erase or export it from inside the app at any time, and uninstalling removes
it.
